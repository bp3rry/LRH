# Third-Party Assets and Data

Everything listed here lives in git-ignored folders (`inputs/`, `assets/`, `out/`) and is
reproduced by the scripts named. CC0 or public-domain sources come first (PLAN.md §4).

| Asset | Source | Licence | Fetched by | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NAIP orthoimagery, 5 Jul 2022, 0.6 m (site) and resampled 3 m (region), R/G/B/NIR | USGS The National Map NAIP ImageServer (`imagery.nationalmap.gov/.../USGSNAIPImagery`) | Public domain (USDA FSA) | `scripts/fetch_imagery.py` → `imagery.lock.json` | Ground albedo; woods/pasture/bare classification |
| Drone photo `Ladonia Arial 1.jpeg` | Owner | Owner's photo | `scripts/import_inputs.py` | 2026 clearing inside the parcels; drone-match check |
| Tree prototypes (post oak, cedar elm, eastern red cedar, bois d'arc; 3 variants each) | Generated by `scripts/blender/trees.py` | Made in this project | build | All trees (instanced) |
| Sky | Blender built-in Multiple Scattering sky | Blender (GPL; renders unencumbered) | render | Sky and sunlight |
| Caption font | macOS Helvetica (`/System/Library/Fonts/Helvetica.ttc`) | Apple system font; used only to render caption text into images | render | §7 caption on marketing frames |

Blender's bundled Python also needs PyYAML, shapely and Pillow, installed into the
git-ignored `.blender-site/` from `scripts/blender/requirements.txt` (see README).
